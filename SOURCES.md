Before publishing complete Current Catholic prompts, obtain applicable written permission or independently document a reusable exact edition. Replace source-linked placeholders only after that review. Private pasted prompts and user audio are not part of source control or the Pages artifact. Users are responsible for choosing material they may record and share. Public-domain status outside the United States can differ.
