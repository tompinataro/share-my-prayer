Prayer wording, sources & privacy

Three wording choices have separate recordings. Traditional Catholic includes verified historical texts where available. Current Catholic links to the USCCB where app reuse permission is unconfirmed; paste a private prompt or read from the source. Contemporary English uses licensed ELLC ecumenical texts and clearly labeled original adaptations. These adaptations are not officially approved Catholic translations.

All Saints: traditional prayers · USCCB: current prayers · USCCB: sequence & mysteries · Church of England: Lord’s Prayer comparison · ELLC: permission · Source register

No accounts, analytics, uploads, AI voices, or default narrator. Sharing sends only the file you choose through your device’s share sheet. Backups contain private audio and should be stored securely.
