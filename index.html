Prayer wording, sources & privacy

Classic prayers combines historical Creed, Hail Mary, Hail Holy Queen and closing wording with an original adapted Fatima prayer. Other prayers retain their customary wording. Current Catholic keeps the earlier words and recordings unchanged. Contemporary English offers licensed ELLC texts and original adaptations, not officially approved Catholic translations. Recordings remain separate for every wording selection.

USCCB: current prayers · USCCB: sequence & mysteries · Church of England: Lord’s Prayer comparison · ELLC: permission · Source register

No accounts, analytics, or recording uploads. Bella AI narration is provided as an optional default; your own saved recording takes precedence. Sharing sends only the file you choose through your device’s share sheet. Backups contain private audio and should be stored securely.

Restore your recordings

Recordings with the same prayer and wording will be replaced. Other recordings and current preferences stay unchanged.
